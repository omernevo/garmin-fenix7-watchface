Your job is to generate a watchface compatible with Garmin Fenix 7. It may work on other Garmin watches but optimization should be done for the Fenix 7.

The template is in a photo in the /prompt folder. Whenever information or instructions are missing, you may simply copy the template.

Design: Copy the template. 
- Seconds are to be shown only when the screen is in active mode (first photo). In rest mode, a line should appear (second photo).

Data fields - generally follow the template.
Circle, starting from top and going clockwise:
- Wind + wind direction from Garmin
- Precipitation + rain in the next hour from OWM
- Total steps in the calendaric week (starting Monday)
- Date (DAY MM D)
- KM cycling this calendaric wek (starting Monday)
- Time in Israel

Fields above time:
-Center top: current temp from OWM
- Left: elevation (with icon on top)
- Center: min/max temp from owm (with icon on top)
- next sun event (with icon on top)

Fields under time:
- left: total intensity min this calendaric week (starting Monday) (with icon on underneath)
- center: current heart rate (with icon on underneath)
- total steps tody (with icon on underneath)
- bottom under heart icon: average resting heart rate past 7 days

Icons: generate graphically similar icons

OWM API: 767c5a6ab53c51d09cbc74d3adc63a3f
